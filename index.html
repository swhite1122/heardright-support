HeardRight

Dictation that gets your names right.

HeardRight is a dictation keyboard for iPhone. You talk, it types, and everything happens on your phone.

What it does

You speak, and the words land in whatever app you're typing in. The speech models run on the phone itself, so your voice never goes to a server and dictation works with no connection at all.

The part that makes it different is the personal dictionary. If dictation keeps writing "Sean" when you mean "Shawn", you tell HeardRight once and it writes it your way from then on. It's built for names, places, and the words only you use.

How pricing works

The app is free to use with a fast, light speech model. A one-time purchase unlocks the most accurate model and the personal dictionary. There is no subscription.
