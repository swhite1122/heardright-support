HeardRight

Dictation that stays on your phone.

HeardRight is a dictation keyboard for iPhone. You tap Dictate, you talk, and the words land where your cursor is.

What it does

It works in Messages, Notes, Mail, and anywhere else you type. The speech models run on the phone itself, so nothing you say is sent to a server, there's no account to make, and once a model is downloaded it works with no connection at all.

You pick a style on the keyboard, and it cleans up what you said. Standard writes clean sentences, Casual keeps your wording like a text message, Formal spells everything out, and Off pastes exactly what was heard. The cleaned-up text is compared against what you said, and if a number changed or the words don't line up, HeardRight pastes your words as heard and tells you why.

The Dictionary is for your names. If dictation keeps writing "Sean" when you mean "Shawn", you add it once and it comes out your way from then on.

How pricing works

HeardRight is free for 14 days with everything included. After that it's a one-time purchase, with no subscription. If you don't unlock it, the keyboard keeps typing and only dictation stops.
