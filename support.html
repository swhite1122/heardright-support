How do I turn the keyboard on?

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Go to General, then Keyboard, then Keyboards.
  3. Tap Add New Keyboard and pick HeardRight.
  4. Tap HeardRight in the list and turn on Allow Full Access.

The app walks you through the same steps the first time you open it.

How do I add the Action Button shortcut?

Open HeardRight, go to Settings, then Action Button, and tap Add Shortcut. If the bundled copy cannot open, you can open the signed shortcut here. Shortcuts will show exactly what it does before you add it.

Why does it need Full Access?

The keyboard and the main app share storage on your phone, and Full Access is what lets the keyboard pick up your finished text from that shared space. It's an Apple requirement for how keyboards work, not a way for us to see your typing. Nothing the keyboard handles gets sent to the internet.

Why do I download a model after installing?

The speech models are big files, so shipping them inside the app would make it a huge download for everyone. Instead the app stays small and you download the model you want, once, from the Models screen. After that, dictation runs entirely on your phone and works offline.

How do I free up space?

Open the Models screen and delete any model you're not using. You can download it again later — nothing else is lost.

Dictation starts but no text shows up

  1. Check that HeardRight has microphone permission in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Microphone.
  2. Make sure a model shows as Active on the Models screen.
  3. Make sure the HeardRight keyboard is the one on screen when you expect the text to land.

If it still won't work, email us with what you tried and we'll figure it out with you.

Refunds and purchases

Purchases go through Apple, so refund requests do too — you can ask at reportaproblem.apple.com. If a purchase didn't unlock what it should have, tap Restore Purchases in the app first, and email us if that doesn't fix it.
