How do I turn the keyboard on?

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone.
  2. Go to General, then Keyboard, then Keyboards.
  3. Tap Add New Keyboard and pick HeardRight.
  4. Tap HeardRight in the list and turn on Allow Full Access.
  5. Open Notes, switch to the HeardRight keyboard once, and go back to HeardRight.

The app walks you through the same steps the first time you open it.

How do I dictate?

  1. Open any app and tap where you type.
  2. Hold the globe key and pick HeardRight.
  3. Tap Dictate.
  4. If HeardRight opens, it is already listening. Swipe right along the bottom edge of the screen to go back to your app.
  5. Talk. Tap the pill to stop, or stop talking and it stops by itself after a few seconds of quiet.

Your words land where the cursor is.

Why does HeardRight open when I tap Dictate?

iOS doesn't let a keyboard use the microphone, so the app has to do the recording. After the first dictation the app stays ready for a few minutes and your next ones start right from the keyboard. You can change how long it stays ready in HeardRight, under the gear, at Armed window.

Why is the orange dot on?

While HeardRight is ready, the microphone is on so a dictation can start without opening the app. The app throws that audio away until you tap Dictate, and nothing you say leaves the phone. A shorter Armed window means the dot goes away sooner.

Why does it need Full Access?

The keyboard and the app share storage on your phone, and Full Access is what lets the keyboard ask the app for a dictation and pick up the finished text. It's how Apple makes keyboards work, not a way for us to see your typing. The keyboard makes no network connections. Without Full Access it still types.

What are the styles?

The chip to the left of Dictate picks how your words are cleaned up. Standard writes clean sentences, Casual keeps your wording like a text message, Formal spells everything out, and Off pastes exactly what was heard. Tap the chip to change it, or hold it to see the list. The styles need the cleanup model, S1-mini, which you download on the Models tab.

Why did it paste my words without cleaning them up?

HeardRight compares the cleaned-up text against what you said. If it doesn't trust the result, it pastes your words as heard, and the entry on the Home tab says why: Numbers changed, Words changed, Too slow, Too long, or Not downloaded. If the cleanup has a problem, you still get your words.

Why do I download a model after installing?

The models are big files, so shipping them inside the app would make it a huge download for everyone. You download the ones you want, once, on the Models tab: Parakeet at 698 MB or Whisper small.en at 257 MB for speech, and S1-mini at 462 MB for the styles. After that, dictation runs entirely on your phone and works offline. HeardRight is English only.

How do I free up space?

Open the Models tab and swipe a model you're not using to delete it. You can download it again later, and nothing else is lost.

Dictation starts but no text shows up

  1. Check that HeardRight has microphone permission in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Microphone.
  2. Make sure a speech model is downloaded on the Models tab.
  3. Make sure the HeardRight keyboard is the one on screen when you expect the text to land.

If it still won't work, email us with what you tried and we'll figure it out with you.

The trial, the purchase, and refunds

HeardRight is free for 14 days. After that, unlocking dictation is a one-time purchase with no subscription, and the keyboard keeps typing either way. If you bought it and it isn't unlocked, open HeardRight, tap the gear, and tap Restore purchase. Purchases go through Apple, so refund requests do too, at reportaproblem.apple.com.
