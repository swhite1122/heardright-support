HeardRight

Privacy Policy

The short version: your voice and your words stay on your phone. We can't see them, and we didn't build a way to.

What the app collects

Nothing. HeardRight has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking of any kind. We do not collect, store, or transmit your recordings, your transcripts, your dictionary, or anything about how you use the app.

Your voice

The microphone records only during a dictation session you started, and the app shows you when it's listening. Every session also stops on its own after a time limit you can see in the app, so the microphone can't run forever. The audio is written to the app's own storage on your phone, transcribed right there by a speech model running on the device, and the audio file is deleted once your text is ready. Audio never leaves the phone.

Your dictionary

The words you teach HeardRight are stored on your phone, in storage that only the app and its keyboard can read. They're applied to your text on the device and never uploaded anywhere.

The keyboard and Full Access

Full Access lets the keyboard read the finished text from the shared storage it splits with the main app. That's the whole reason it's needed. The keyboard makes no network connections and sends nothing off the device.

The one time the app touches the network

When you choose to download a speech model, the app fetches that file from Hugging Face, the site that hosts them. Like any download, that request shows the hosting server standard connection information such as your IP address. No personal data, audio, or text goes with it, and after the download, dictation works with no connection at all.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

HeardRight doesn't collect data from anyone, children included, and has no features directed at children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, we don't expect much to change.
